# Dependencies

No runtime npm dependencies. Vite, TypeScript, Playwright, and Prettier are development tools. har-schema 2.0.0 (ISC), Ajv 8.20.0 (MIT), and ajv-formats 3.0.1 (MIT) validate the generated HAR during tests; they are not shipped to browsers.

See package.json and package-lock.json for exact versions. No third-party fonts, analytics, or remote images are loaded by the application. Example data and the preview image are generated from fictional fixtures in this repository.
